Decisions

Open questions and who owns each one.

  • Option A or Option B?

    Both couples, open

    The difference is pace and length; both hit every must-see.

  • Both rigs

    Both couples, settled

    Thorsens: 24 ft trailer, 46 ft combined, 30 A. Volskys: 25 ft 11 in trailer, truck 20 ft 11 in plus hitch (about 48 ft combined), 30 A. Neither rig needs 50 A; every candidate is judged against the Volskys' longer trailer.

  • Acadia — dry camp in the park, or hookups outside it?

    Both couples, open

    Blackwoods and Seawall have no hookups at any site, so 3–4 nights there is dry camping. Only about 30 sites in each take trailers over 25 ft. Bar Harbor Campground has full hookups. Schoodic Woods caps sites at 25 ft — too short for the Volskys' trailer.

  • Border crossing into Ontario

    Thorsens, open

    Fastest routing goes Detroit → Windsor by bridge (Ambassador or Gordie Howe). Buffalo's Peace Bridge is the alternative. The Detroit–Windsor tunnel restricts propane — plan on a bridge.

  • Rally to Niagara is a 400-mile day with a border crossing

    Both couples, open

    About 9 hours towing. Option A's spare night could split it with an overnight near London, Ontario. Edmundston to Fundy (about 280 mi) is the next-longest day.

  • Rally early-arrival date

    Thorsens, open

    Plan assumes arrival Fri June 23. Adjust once ACI posts 2028 parking and early-arrival rules.

  • Québec City — south shore (KOA) or north shore (Charlesbourg)?

    Both couples, open

  • Canada Day (Sat July 1) is a travel day

    Both couples, noted

    Niagara → Thousand Islands. Expect holiday traffic and full campgrounds; book early.

  • Québec Construction Holiday dates for 2028

    Thorsens, open

    Usually the last two weeks of July. Confirm 2028 dates when published — they drive how early to book the Maritimes and Acadia.

  • Wood Islands–Caribou ferry with two trailers

    Thorsens, open

    Confirm length limits, propane rules and whether reservations are needed for the PEI exit.

  • Key booking dates

    Thorsens, noted

    Acadia NPS campgrounds: 10 am ET Jan 1, 2028 for July arrivals (90% of sites release six months ahead on the first of each month). Parks Canada (Fundy, PEI, Cape Breton Highlands): early February 2028 — the 2026 season opened Feb 6 (Fundy) and Feb 9 (PEI, Cape Breton). Private parks: check each; most open about a year out.
