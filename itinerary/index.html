Itinerary

Holds the 5-week limit with 1 spare night. Mostly 2–3 night stops — a travel-heavy pace with little room for weather. 34 nights, 3,699 towing miles, home Thu Jul 27, 2028.

  1. 1Jun 23–Jun 296 nights

    ACI International Rally Celina, OH

    77 mi from home, about 2.0 h towing

    71st Airstream Club International Rally, June 24–29, 2028, Mercer County Fairgrounds. ACI assigns rally parking.

    • Rally seminars, caravan and club meetups
    • Grand Lake St. Marys, a few minutes from the fairgrounds
    • Airstream factory in Jackson Center (about 35 mi) — check whether rally-week factory tours are offered

    Campground: Mercer County Fairgrounds (rally parking)

  2. 2Jun 29–Jul 12 nights

    Niagara Falls Niagara Falls, ON

    406 mi from ACI International Rally, about 9.2 h towing

    Canadian side only — best views of Horseshoe Falls.

    • Boat ride into the Horseshoe Falls basin (Niagara City Cruises, Canadian side)
    • Journey Behind the Falls
    • Niagara Parkway drive to Niagara-on-the-Lake — wineries, old town, Shaw Festival
    • WEGO bus instead of parking the truck at the falls

    Campground: Niagara Falls KOA Holiday; Campark Resorts

  3. 3Jul 1–Jul 21 night

    Thousand Islands Ivy Lea / Gananoque, ON

    265 mi from Niagara Falls, about 6.1 h towing

    St. Lawrence islands; Kingston is 30 minutes west.

    • Island boat cruise from Gananoque or Rockport
    • Boldt Castle (Heart Island, US side — passport needed from the Canadian boats)
    • Fort Henry National Historic Site, Kingston
    • Thousand Islands Parkway bike path

    Campground: 1000 Islands / Ivy Lea KOA

  4. 4Jul 2–Jul 53 nights

    Québec City Québec, QC

    306 mi from Thousand Islands, about 7.3 h towing

    Walled old city on the St. Lawrence.

    • Old Québec — walls, Château Frontenac, Dufferin Terrace
    • Plains of Abraham
    • Montmorency Falls
    • Île d'Orléans loop (farm stands, cideries)

    Campground: KOA Québec City (Saint-Nicolas, south shore); Camping de la Joie (Charlesbourg)

  5. 5Jul 5–Jul 61 night

    Edmundston (overnight) Saint-Jacques, NB

    188 mi from Québec City, about 4.7 h towing

    One-night break on the long Québec-to-Fundy run.

    • Rest and resupply

    Campground: Parc provincial de la République

  6. 6Jul 6–Jul 93 nights

    Fundy National Park Alma, NB

    282 mi from Edmundston (overnight), about 6.7 h towing

    World's highest tides. Plan activities around the tide table.

    • Hopewell Rocks — walk the ocean floor at low tide, come back at high tide
    • Fundy Trail Parkway
    • Cape Enrage
    • Dickson Falls; lobster in Alma

    Campground: Headquarters Campground (Parks Canada)

  7. 7Jul 9–Jul 123 nights

    Prince Edward Island Cavendish, PE

    147 mi from Fundy National Park, about 4.3 h towing

    In over the Confederation Bridge, out on the Wood Islands ferry — no backtracking, and the bridge toll is paid only on leaving.

    • Green Gables Heritage Place
    • PEI National Park beaches and dunes
    • Charlottetown — Province House, waterfront
    • Community lobster supper (New Glasgow area)

    Campground: Cavendish Campground (PEI National Park); Cavendish KOA Holiday

  8. 8Jul 12–Jul 153 nights

    Cape Breton Baddeck, NS

    205 mi from Prince Edward Island, about 5.4 h towing plus the Wood Islands–Caribou ferry (about 75 min crossing)

    Base camp for the Cabot Trail. Leave the trailers; drive it in the trucks.

    • Cabot Trail loop by truck
    • Skyline Trail, Cape Breton Highlands NP
    • Alexander Graham Bell National Historic Site, Baddeck
    • Fortress of Louisbourg day trip

    Campground: Baddeck Cabot Trail Campground; Broad Cove Campground (Cape Breton Highlands NP)

  9. 9Jul 15–Jul 183 nights

    Halifax & South Shore Halifax, NS

    209 mi from Cape Breton, about 5.2 h towing

    The turnaround point.

    • Halifax Citadel National Historic Site
    • Waterfront boardwalk and Pier 21
    • Peggy's Cove lighthouse
    • Lunenburg (UNESCO old town) and Mahone Bay

    Campground: Shubie Campground (Dartmouth); Graves Island Provincial Park (Chester)

  10. 10Jul 18–Jul 191 night

    Saint John (overnight) Saint John, NB

    252 mi from Halifax & South Shore, about 5.8 h towing

    Splits the Halifax-to-Acadia run; the St. Stephen–Calais border comes next morning.

    • Reversing Falls
    • Saint John City Market

    Campground: Rockwood Park Campground

  11. 11Jul 19–Jul 223 nights

    Acadia National Park Mount Desert Island, ME

    193 mi from Saint John (overnight), about 5.4 h towing

    Park the rigs and ride the free Island Explorer shuttle — the Park Loop Road isn't for trailers.

    • Park Loop Road on the Island Explorer shuttle
    • Cadillac Mountain sunrise (vehicle reservation on recreation.gov)
    • Jordan Pond House popovers; carriage roads
    • Bass Harbor Head Light; Schoodic Peninsula

    Campground: Blackwoods Campground (NPS); Seawall Campground (NPS); Bar Harbor Campground

  12. 12Jul 22–Jul 242 nights

    White Mountains Glen / North Conway, NH

    208 mi from Acadia National Park, about 6.2 h towing

    Two nights in the notches before the run home.

    • Kancamagus Highway by truck
    • Conway Scenic Railroad
    • Mount Washington Cog Railway or Auto Road (truck only)
    • Flume Gorge, Franconia Notch

    Campground: Glen Ellis Family Campground

  13. 13Jul 24–Jul 251 night

    Lake George (overnight) Lake George, NY

    219 mi from White Mountains, about 6.2 h towing

    Drive home, night 1.

    • Steamboat cruise; Fort William Henry

    Campground: Not chosen yet

  14. 14Jul 25–Jul 261 night

    Finger Lakes (overnight) Romulus, NY

    227 mi from Lake George (overnight), about 5.8 h towing

    Drive home, night 2.

    • Seneca Lake wineries; Watkins Glen gorge trail

    Campground: Sampson State Park

  15. 15Jul 26–Jul 271 night

    Erie (overnight) Erie, PA

    220 mi from Finger Lakes (overnight), about 5.5 h towing

    Drive home, night 3.

    • Presque Isle State Park

    Campground: Erie KOA Holiday; Sara's Campground
